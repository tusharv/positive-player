Includes Mile Sur Mera Tumhara (FAR Commercials), Ek Anek Aur Ekta (Films Division), the 1987 Freedom Run (Prasar Bharati Archives), Baje Sargam, Tree of Unity, the literacy campaign Purab Se Surya Uga, DD signature montage and news theme, helmet safety and anti-smoking spots. Ads span transport, clothing, electronics, food, drink, household goods, personal care and health.

Selections are interleaved across the four categories. The existing daily rotation applies to this shorter catalogue. Original short ad lengths are preserved. Public/processed/embeddable status, positive duration and India region eligibility were checked through YouTube metadata; playback availability can still change or differ by region. Vintage TV advertising is not uniformly documented as having aired on DD; this is an era-based selection, not a broadcast-history assertion for every spot.

The authoritative selection is `src/data/programs/013.json`, imported by `HAND_PICKED_PROGRAMS['Vintage India']` to preserve it during bundle regeneration. The manifest version changes with this catalogue so the old Kindness cache is not reused. DD Classics remains unchanged by this replacement.
